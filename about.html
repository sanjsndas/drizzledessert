    • We are Drizzle.

    An independent dessert publication for people who care about texture, balance, and the quiet craft of finishing well.

    From the founding editor

    Maya Rivera

    I started Drizzle after years of baking in a small apartment kitchen, scribbling notes on the backs of envelopes. I wanted a place where technique wasn’t rushed, where a recipe could be read like a short story, and where the science of sugar was treated with the same reverence as a sourdough starter.

    Every article you read here has been tested at least twice — usually more. We don’t publish anything we wouldn’t serve to our own families. And we never chase trends; we chase the perfect crumb, the glossy ganache, the caramel that sits right on the edge of bitter.

    — Maya
    We believe dessert is not decoration. It is craft — and it deserves the same rigour as any other discipline.
    The Drizzle editorial team

    What we stand for

    Three principles guide every recipe, guide, and essay we publish.

    01

    Tested twice, always

    No recipe leaves our kitchen until it has been baked, chilled, or caramelised at least twice — once to develop, once to verify. We note the variables so you can adapt with confidence.

    02

    Technique over trend

    We’re not interested in the latest viral hack. We’re interested in why a fold works, how temperature changes texture, and what a recipe teaches you about craft.

    03

    Generous, not gatekeeping

    Every guide is written to be understood by a home baker with a modest kitchen. We explain the “why” so you can improvise, substitute, and make it yours.

    How we got here

    2019

    A notebook and a rented oven

    Maya begins documenting caramel experiments on a food blog called “The Drizzle.” It’s read by eleven people, most of them friends.

    2021

    Elias joins, and the test kitchen doubles

    Elias Vance, a pastry chef with a obsession for dough, comes on board. Together they formalise the twice-tested rule and start publishing long-form technique guides.

    2023

    Drizzle Dessert House is born

    The blog becomes a full publication. Nadia K. joins to lead frozen desserts and chocolate work. The first print-style digital issue is released to 4,000 subscribers.

    2025

    Still independent, still testing

    We publish weekly, remain ad-free, and continue to test every recipe in a home kitchen. Our archive now holds over 120 guides.
